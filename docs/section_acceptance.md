## Next sequence of explicit exit gates

1. Convert confirmed visual-frontier coverage into effective goal-directed learning on held-out tasks (key, switch, altered, hidden) and measure both control success and model-prediction efficiency; do not mistake movement coverage for task mastery.
2. Replicate controlled skill composition at varying observation noise and modified rules, then integrate context-conditioned options with the procedural gridworld and measure transfer success with matched interactions.
3. Section 09 now meets the controlled primary-environment gate for competing mechanisms, uncertainty-aware intervention selection, and failure to identify. Broader causal generalization remains a research direction.
4. Demonstrate a curriculum/meta-policy advantage with isolated, multi-seed ablations and comparable resource budgets.
5. Move verifier into a genuinely isolated execution boundary with verifier-private evaluation assets, protected results, resource limits and a statistically pre-registered promotion policy.
6. Run a fresh, independently held-out two-generation improvement study; a failed result must remain failed.

No milestone is automatically closed just because an experiment was attempted.
