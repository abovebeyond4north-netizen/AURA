# Known limitations — must not be represented as successes

1. **Partially implemented rather than proven:** world-model prediction trained and evaluable, curiosity action choice, Bayesian action-effect hypotheses, noisy observations, image-input encoder, replay, evidence provenance, lightweight curriculum, cost-sensitive UCB meta-controller, typed modifications, paired verifier and archive.
2. **Simplified and weak:** world dynamics recurrent one-step with imagined latent rollout, not a fully probabilistic RSSM; no calibrated density or out-of-distribution detector; no demonstrated multi-horizon advantage. Hidden mechanism changes may be unidentifiable from local observations.
3. **Not yet established:** independently validated skill discovery/transfer, useful autonomous concept abstraction, retention across distribution shift, noisy-TV benchmark superiority, causal discovery in the general case, learning-strategy search advantage, two accepted successive improvements. Skill discovery requires repeated successful episodes; sparse rewards may yield none.
4. **Experimental design:** bootstrap gate is not corrected for selection and repeated testing; image-noise penalty approximates aleatoric error; initial local validation is sampled from early replay and held fixed but could lack representative diversity.
5. **Security:** subprocess verifier only protects against permitted interfaces and accidental mutation, not arbitrary malicious OS code running with the same permissions. SQLite hash chain is tamper evident assuming final trusted hash; not tamper-proof against DB replacement. Require real sandbox, externally controlled signatures, external test seeds and resource quotas in production.
6. **Package installation:** environment used for development cannot reach PyPI; Gymnasium and TensorBoard are not available in the local runtime. Optional TensorBoard writer and Python 3.11/3.12 compatibility have not been verified here.
7. **Engineering completeness:** most of the requested research subsystems have a bounded algorithm but broad 'every module fully proven' acceptance criteria remain unmet. The five baseline conditions have not been statistically characterized; robust results require more resources and separate experimental campaigns.

8. **Expanded multiscale model:** Residual multi-horizon heads outperform copy-current-frame on additive visual dynamics, but the same benefit has *not* been demonstrated on full procedural tasks. Latent effects are learned representation predictions, not automatically identifiable causal mechanisms or symbolic abstractions.
9. **Expanded uncertainty:** Repeated conditional image variance is observable, not proof of truly irreducible noise; unobserved state can produce similar variation. Conformal-style frozen image shift thresholds are only empirical under matched reference/calibration distributions and do not generalize to arbitrary domain shift. Small smoke-run ensembles and uncorrected temporal dependencies preclude calibrated epistemic probabilities.
